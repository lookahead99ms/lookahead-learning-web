Reference · The agreed plan catalog and where learners buy. Local mock payments only.

Local mock payments. The server stores purchases and catalog access. No money is charged. Existing course grants remain separate.

How to review

  1. Look for the ways in: the plans section on the home page (before the Essence), Start free → What you’re missing, and the lock screen on a locked lesson. Signed in, also Choose a plan in Manage account. The header has no pricing link.
  2. Start a sale on Sales and prices, then open Pricing: the sale is on top and the regular plans are read-only. The server publishes it during its configured dates.
  3. Open Pricing and select a Choose … button to review and confirm a simulated purchase.
  4. Open Manage account → Membership. The sample below shows what a buyer would see.
  5. Compare viewers. Your staff account sees everything: every plan, including sale plans (marked staff-only), and the full Start free page. A signed-in learner sees only what fits: "Your plan", "Upgrade to …", and smaller plans "available from" the last 7 days of their plan.
  6. Check the configured plans and the change rules.
