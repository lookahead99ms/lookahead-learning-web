Reference · The agreed plan catalog and where learners buy. Nothing here is sold yet.

Design only. Payment is not connected and the server has no plans or purchases yet, so nobody can buy and nothing is charged. Access still comes from course grants set on People and access.

How to review

  1. Look for the ways in: the plans section on the home page (before the Essence), Start free → What you’re missing, and the lock screen on a locked lesson. Signed in, also Choose a plan in Manage account. The header has no pricing link.
  2. Start a sale preview on Sales and prices, then open Pricing: the sale is on top and the regular plans are read-only. Only you see it.
  3. Open Pricing and select a Choose … button to see the checkout notice.
  4. Open Manage account → Membership. Your staff account has no purchases, so it shows your access, not a plan; the sample below shows what a buyer would see.
  5. Check the configured plans and the change rules.
