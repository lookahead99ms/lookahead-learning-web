People and access

Give people the access they need.

Only the owner changes roles and course access. A change applies the next time the person loads a page, and never shows anyone another learner’s plans or progress.

  • 🧑‍💼

    Staff roles

    Co-authors read the author tools. Admins manage test accounts. Both get the entire curriculum.

  • 📚

    Course access grants

    Open chosen courses, or the entire curriculum, for one person. A grant is not a purchase and costs nothing.

  • 🏷️

    Plans and purchases

    Local purchases use mock payments, so no real money is charged.

    See plans and purchases
